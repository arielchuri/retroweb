⚡ Manifesto: Why We Ban JavaScript
The modern web is bloated with multi-megabyte frameworks, invasive tracking pixels, cookie consent popups, and algorithmic feeds. RetroWeb goes back to the golden era: pure HTML and CSS hand-crafted by real people.

  • Strict No-JS Rule: Upload only clean HTML, CSS, images, and text. Fast, private, accessible.
  • Instant Subdomain: Your site is published at username.retroweb.site.
  • Built-in Discovery: Get featured in our Community Directory and the Random Site Surfer.
